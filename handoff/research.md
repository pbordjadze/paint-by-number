WHERE THE TWO FAILURE MODES ARISE (measured on Parrots)
1. Slivers: nearest-paint labelling of soft feather texture and blurred edges leaves many regions of 30–150 px whose colour differs from a neighbouring paint by only 1–3 JND, plus "transition strips" a few pixels wide along every blurred edge (the beak's outline, the eye ring) that are labelled with a third, intermediate paint. They pass the old area/inscribed-disc rules and each gets a number.
2. Banding: at 150 colours the palette spacing is ~0.018 OKLab, so a bokeh highlight becomes 12 nested rings whose boundaries have almost no image contrast (mean pixel step across the boundary is 5–15% of the paint difference, versus 30–60% at real contours). At 24 colours the same highlight has 4–5 rings whose paints are 0.07–0.14 apart (3–7 JND): that is a palette-allocation effect (five neutral paints spent on a structureless background), not something a merge rule can fix without visibly wrong colour.

WHAT CHANGED (Sources/PaintCore/Segmentation)
- RegionSimplifier.mergeRound: merge key = relative area × contrast factor, where the factor is min(max(distance of the region's mean colour to the closest neighbouring paint / crumbContrast, crumbFloor), 1) with crumbContrast 0.12, crumbFloor 0.2: a crumb at no contrast needs 5× the minimum area, a distinct spot (eye, highlight) keeps the old threshold. The area minimum is floored at π·minRadius² so the rule has a footing at detail 1. Transition strips: a region is a strip when its compactness 16A/P² < 0.35 and mean width 2A/P < 3·minRadius, its two longest borders own ≥ 50% of its perimeter (the second ≥ 15%), its mean colour projects inside the segment between those two neighbours' paints (t in 0.15…0.85) within 0.4× its distance to the nearer paint, and the mean pixel step across each of those borders is < 0.7× the paint difference. Strips merge whatever their area; sharp-edged or own-coloured thin features fail one test and stay.
- BandMerging (new, after simplification, before texture consolidation): adjacent regions fuse, closest paints first, when the boundary is weak (mean pixel step < 0.25× the paint difference) and the total paint spread of everything fused stays within a tolerance: bandNearTolerance = 0.045·lerp(1.3, 0.8, detail) anywhere (paints too close to tell apart), bandTolerance = 0.1·lerp(1.4, 0.7, detail) for narrow bands (mean width ≤ 2% of the frame side), shrinking to the near tolerance with importance. Fused regions are unions, so every size and radius guarantee holds; absorbed bands' pixels are rewritten in the reference labelling so the palette refit centres the paint on the whole ramp.
- RegionAdjacency.boundarySteps: colour step summed along every shared border, parallel over fixed row chunks added in order.
- SegmentationParameters documents every new tunable with its rationale; Segmenter's pipeline overview and RegionSimplifier's doc comment updated; CLAUDE.md gains the determinism rule for parallel float reductions; .gitignore matches tools/node_modules when it is a symlink (it is one in worktrees).
