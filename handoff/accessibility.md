Minors: VoiceOver three-finger scrolling no longer pans the canvas (add `accessibilityScroll`), undo/pulse/bump still animate under Reduce Motion, and the last commit lacks a body. 1. [major] App/PaintByNumberUITests/AccessibilityUITests.swift:53
   `first.tap()` on a virtual `UIAccessibilityElement` goes through XCUITest's hittability check, which hit-tests the element's centre and requires the same element to come back. The canvas exposes up to 40 areas whose frames are at least 44 pt square while their anchors (region numbers) can be only a few points apart at fit zoom (parrots at ~0.5 pt/px), so the frames of neighbouring areas overlap and the hit test at `first`'s centre can resolve to a neighbour. When that happens XCTest fails the tap with "not hittable" and `testCanvasOffersUnpaintedAreas` fails on CI even though the product behaves correctly (VoiceOver activates via `accessibilityActivate`, not a hit test). Plausible rather than confirmed here (no simulator), but the fix is strictly safer and costs nothing.
   Suggested fix: Tap by coordinate instead of by element so no hittability check is involved: replace `first.tap()` with `first.coordinate(withNormalizedOffset: CGPoint(x: 0.5, y: 0.5)).tap()` (the frame is centred on the region's number by construction, see `CanvasView.areaFrame`). Keep the existing `app.buttons[id].exists` / `query.count` assertions.

2. [minor] App/PaintByNumber/Canvas/CanvasView.swift:852
   Overriding `accessibilityElements` removes the `UIScrollView` from the accessibility tree, so VoiceOver's three-finger swipe (`accessibilityScroll`) no longer pans or pages the canvas; the old `allowsDirectInteraction` path did allow it. VoiceOver users can only move the camera through the custom actions (Zoom to next area / Zoom to fit / Hint), which is what the brief asked for, but a whole-canvas exploration by paging is the standard VoiceOver idiom for scrollable content and is cheap to add.
   Suggested fix: Override `accessibilityScroll(_ direction: UIAccessibilityScrollDirection) -> Bool` on `CanvasView`: compute the chrome-free area, move the camera by one visible page (`animateCamera(zoom: scrollView.zoomScale, offset: clampedOffset(contentOffset ± page, zoom:), duration: 0.3)`), set `pendingFocus = .first`, return true when the offset actually changed (false at the edges so VoiceOver plays the boundary sound). `cameraDidSettle` already rebuilds and focuses the first area.

3. [minor] App/PaintByNumber/Canvas/CanvasView.swift:697
   Under Reduce Motion fills, the shine and the replay are stepped, but undo (`session(_:didUnpaint:)`) still schedules a 0.25 s fade-out, `reveal(region:)` still pulses the revealed region for ~2 s and `bump` still wiggles a rejected region. The fade is harmless, but the pulse and bump are motion; the brief's item 4 ('fills complete instantly, no shine sweep, replay steps') is met, this is consistency polish.
   Suggested fix: In `session(_:didUnpaint:)` use `renderer.update(r, .settled(painted: false, origin: Self.labelPosition(template, r), seed: seed))` when `reduceMotion`; in `reveal(region:)` skip `pulseRegion`/`pulseStart` when `reduceMotion` (VoiceOver focus already lands on the area), and in `bump` return early under `reduceMotion` (the swatch shake is already suppressed in `PaintChromeState`).
