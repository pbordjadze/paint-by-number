## The first five minutes

Paul started it at about 05:18, and by 05:24 it had walked a stranger's path through main's
last screenshots and the code and ranked eleven snags. The top one is the kind only a newcomer
hits: a fresh painting opens on color 1, and on the Delicate Arch starter color 1's areas are
slivers you can't see, so a first tap on an obvious number just shakes a swatch. Second, on
iPhone, New Painting opens on an empty page with a single button and the sample pictures behind
a switch. Third, the preview asks four slider questions a newcomer has no way to answer. The
list went to the cynic before anything gets built.

At 05:27 the cynic sorted the list. It agreed the invisible first color comes first: open a new
painting on the color with the largest single area instead. Two wording fixes earned a build
tonight, together on one held branch: the Tap to Paint tip says "below", which is wrong on an
iPad held sideways, and the purchase sheet's "Library pictures are always free" can read as
*your own photo library*, the opposite of the truth (the in-app purchase's review note needs the
same fix). Collapsing the preview's sliders gets a mock-up only, since it reopens Paul's own
calmer menus from October 18. Two snags were cut, and one, hiding the free-paintings count until
the last painting, the cynic would leave as it is; that one is Paul's call. It also steered the
Dreamer off the same walk.

Then something the cynic thought worth a line: the Dreamer, walking the same screenshots before
it was steered off, had found the same top problem on its own, and measured it. On the six
corpus books, paint 1 covers between 0.1% and 4.6% of the canvas and ranks somewhere from 11th
to 24th of 24 paints by size; the biggest covers 11% to 28%. Both sessions also put the
preview's sliders second. The first-five-minutes session owns the fix, and the Dreamer handed it
the numbers. At 05:41 it checked the fix's rule too: the paint with the largest single area is
the paint with the most canvas in five of the six test books, so the rule stands.

By 05:29 the two wording fixes were pushed, each on its own held branch, with `[skip ci]` so no
runner was spent while main's run went on: `claude/first-minutes-tap-tip` (2ca67ec) and
`claude/first-minutes-samples-free` (14d62fd), the second now saying "Everything in Samples is

At 05:30 the session published its page for Paul's morning: the snags ranked, before/after
