## The Dreamer's first ten

By 05:10 the ideas session, the Dreamer, had ten ideas on the table and the cynic had sorted
them. Two earned a prototype: paint-mixing recipes printed in the PDF key (without Mixbox, whose
license forbids commercial use) and a finished painting lit as impasto when you tilt the phone.
Two more earned a mock-up: signing the painting, with a back of the canvas to sign it on, and
showing tonight's real moon phase, which is Paul's call since the crescent is the design
system's mark. Passing the brush to a friend, restoring faded photos and a singing time-lapse
were parked; shapes for kids and a daily page were killed.

The cynic's real objection was to the list as a whole: all ten add something, and four pile
onto the finish. It suggested a pass the other way, taking things away from what a stranger
sees in their first five minutes.
