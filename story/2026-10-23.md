## The Dreamer's first ten

By 05:10 the ideas session, the Dreamer, had ten ideas on the table and the cynic had sorted
them. Two earned a prototype: paint-mixing recipes printed in the PDF key (without Mixbox, whose
license forbids commercial use) and a finished painting lit as impasto when you tilt the phone.
Two more earned a mock-up: signing the painting, with a back of the canvas to sign it on, and
showing tonight's real moon phase, which is Paul's call since the crescent is the design
system's mark. Passing the brush to a friend, restoring faded photos and a singing time-lapse
were parked; shapes for kids and a daily page were killed.

The cynic's real objection was to the list as a whole: all ten add something, and four pile
onto the finish. It suggested a pass the other way, taking things away from what a stranger
sees in their first five minutes.

## The first results

At 05:20 the Dreamer came back with two prototypes. The mixing recipes claimed a median error
of 0.8 ΔE, which the cynic took apart: that's the solver agreeing with its own model of paint,
not real tubes on real paper, so it stays untested until someone mixes real swatches. Its new
framing, "Make a kit", a printable kit with a shopping list of tubes, is a product decision for
Paul.

The tilt-lit brushwork changed shape. It now lives in the last two seconds of the time-lapse,
where the finished painting catches the light: no motion sensors, no clash with Nightfall. The
cynic called it the best idea of the night so far, and left Paul one question: is brushwork
texture the painter never painted a gift, or a fib? It asked for stills to judge it. Both prototypes run off the device, made from pbn's coloring
books, with no branch and no CI. The Dreamer's next steps: a morning page with Build, Park or
Drop beside each idea, and, taking up the cynic's challenge, its own walk through a stranger's
first five minutes to find one thing to take away.

Around 05:15 Paul offered one more session. The record keeper suggested the other direction
from the Dreamer's: a session that walks a stranger's first five minutes and proposes what to
take away, paired with the cynic. Paul asked for its prompt to start himself.
