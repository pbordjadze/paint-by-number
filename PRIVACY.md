# Paint by Moonlight: Privacy Policy

*Last updated October 22, 2026*

Paint by Moonlight turns your photos into paint-by-numbers paintings on your iPhone or iPad. It
has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only thing it can send off your device
is feedback about a painting, and only when you tap Send. This page explains what that means in
practice.

## What the app collects

Nothing, unless you choose to send feedback. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no
tracking and no third-party SDKs. The app never sends your photos, your paintings or anything
about you or your device to us or to anyone else, except what you share yourself through the share
sheet and when you tap Send in Give Feedback: then the feedback you reviewed is uploaded to the
developers (see Sharing and feedback below).

That feedback is the only data the app collects. It is not linked to you (there is no account to
link it to), it is not used for tracking, and it is used only to fix problems in the app. The
app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and this collected data, for the app's own functions:
your notes (customer support), the app version, device model and system version with the
painting's settings (other diagnostic data), and your painting with your marks, plus your
original photo only if you switch it on (photos).

## Your photos and paintings

- **Turning a photo into a painting happens entirely on your device**, including the on-device
  models that draw its lines.
- **Photos.** You pick photos with Apple's photo picker, which runs separately from the app; the
  app only receives the photo you choose. It doesn't ask to read your photo library.
- **Camera.** The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo for a new painting.
  The photo stays on your device.
- **Saving to Photos.** The app adds a painting or its time-lapse to your photo library only when
  you choose Save Image or Save Video while sharing it.
- **Storage.** Your paintings, their progress and the photos they were made from are kept in the
  app's own storage on your device. They are included in your device's backups like any app's
  data, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

## Unlimited Paintings

Three paintings of your own photos are free, and a free one comes back each day, up to three at
a time; Unlimited Paintings, a one-time purchase, lifts that limit. You buy it from Apple through
the App Store, under Apple's terms: Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account, and we never
receive your name, email address or payment details (Apple's sales reports to developers count
purchases without saying who made them). The app checks on your device whether you have bought
it, and counts your free paintings on your device. Nothing about it is sent to us.

## Sharing and feedback

Nothing leaves your device unless you send or share it yourself. When you share a painting, print
it, export a time-lapse or send a template, you choose where it goes with Apple's share sheet.

**Send Template** makes a file of a painting for someone else to paint: its blank template (with
any changes you made to the drawing), its title and the settings that made it. It includes the
original photo unless you switch Original Photo off. It never includes your progress. The file
goes only where you send it; it doesn't pass through us.

**Give Feedback** puts together a report that you can read before sending. It contains your
painting with the marks you drew on it and a close-up of each, your notes, the painting's
settings and progress, and the app version, device model and system version. Your original photo
is included only if you switch it on (a painting made from one of the app's own pictures names
the picture instead).

- **Sending.** When you tap Send, the report is uploaded straight to the developers through a
  relay running on Cloudflare, and kept in a private repository on GitHub that only the
  developers can read.
- **Not linked to you.** The app has no accounts and asks for no name, email address or contact
  details, so a report is not tied to who you are. What you write in your notes is up to you:
  please leave out anything you wouldn't want us to read.
- **Your connection.** As with any request over the internet, the upload reaches Cloudflare from
  your IP address. The relay uses it only to limit how quickly one connection can send reports,
  and does not save it with the report.
- **Use.** The developers use a report only to see what went wrong with a painting and to fix it.
  It is not sold, shared for advertising or used for anything else.
- **Your choice.** Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. If sending fails you can try again, share
  the report some other way (for example Mail, Messages or AirDrop), or cancel, which keeps what
  you wrote.

## TestFlight

If you test Paint by Moonlight through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and
any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight
terms. Feedback you send from inside the app goes to us as described above, not through
TestFlight.

## Children

The app has no accounts and asks for no personal details from anyone, including children. The
only thing it can send is the feedback described above, and only when its user taps Send.
