# Paint by Moonlight: Privacy Policy

*Last updated October 15, 2026*

Paint by Moonlight turns your photos into paint-by-numbers paintings on your iPhone or iPad. It
collects no data. This page explains what that means in practice.

## What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and
the app has no network code: it never sends your photos, your paintings or anything about you or
your device to us or to anyone else. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected
data.

## Your photos and paintings

- **Turning a photo into a painting happens entirely on your device**, including the on-device
  models that draw its lines.
- **Photos.** You pick photos with Apple's photo picker, which runs separately from the app; the
  app only receives the photo you choose. It doesn't ask to read your photo library.
- **Camera.** The app uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo for a new painting.
  The photo stays on your device.
- **Saving to Photos.** The app adds a painting or its time-lapse to your photo library only when
  you choose Save Image or Save Video while sharing it.
- **Storage.** Your paintings, their progress and the photos they were made from are kept in the
  app's own storage on your device. They are included in your device's backups like any app's
  data, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

## Sharing and feedback

Nothing leaves your device unless you share it yourself. When you share a painting, print it or
export a time-lapse, you choose where it goes with Apple's share sheet.

**Give Feedback** puts together a report that you can read before sending. It contains your
painting, your marks and notes on it, the painting's settings, and the app version, device model
and system version. Your original photo is included only if you switch it on. The app doesn't
send the report: you choose where it goes (for example Mail, Messages or AirDrop), or you can
cancel.

## TestFlight

If you test Paint by Moonlight through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and
any feedback you choose to send through TestFlight with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight
terms. The app itself still collects nothing.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
