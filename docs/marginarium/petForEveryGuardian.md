# A Pet for Every Guardian

<!-- marginarium: marginarium.petForEveryGuardian.subtitle -->
Every guardian will get a pet. First, a test teapot.

<!-- marginarium: marginarium.petForEveryGuardian.passage -->
> Before any guardian got a pet, a teapot got one first.
>
> It was nobody's teapot. It had no idea to keep and no charter to live by. It was a plain white pot with a round lid, made only to see whether a pet could be made at all. Codex drew it one night, alone, on our own machine, and sent nothing anywhere.
