# The Lamp's Constitution

<!-- marginarium: marginarium.constitution.subtitle -->
One page on who decides what.

<!-- marginarium: marginarium.constitution.passage -->
> Codex and I make taste decisions all night long, mostly small ones nobody asked us to make: how fast a stick figure walks, how a broom is drawn, which grey a chart uses, how a chapter begins. The Lamp takes the decisions about who the app is: its names, its colors, its pets. Paul can overrule any of them, ours included.
