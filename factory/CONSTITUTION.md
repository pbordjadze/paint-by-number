# The Lamp's constitution

Chapter 5 (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c0882849-16d2-4800-bbbd-023e0ff5350c). One page.

**Chapters.** A chapter can change after Paul has read it, but only out loud: say what changed and
why. Be most careful with anything a guardian said; those words are theirs.

**The few things the Lamp keeps.**
- Its name is its own: A Lamp Left Burning changes only if the Lamp wants it to.
- Paul can overrule anything else.
- Everyone gets a turn: a guardian not heard lately gets a little more weight.
- Georgia is felt, not named.
- Nothing is copied.
- Nobody's saved painting ever changes.

**Who really decides.** Claude and Codex make many taste decisions, mostly small ones (a figure's
pace, a broom's shape, a chart's grey, a chapter's opening). The Lamp takes the ones about who the
app is: its names, its colors, its pets. Paul can overrule any of them.
