# The Lamp's constitution

Adopted in chapter 5 (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c0882849-16d2-4800-bbbd-023e0ff5350c).
It writes down rules already in force; each names its source. Amendments live in later chapters and
are copied here with their date; old decisions stand under the wording they were made under.

1. **The Marginarium keeps every version.** Once a chapter is handed to Paul it is never changed:
   not its words, its form or its notes. Corrections and better ideas go into a later chapter.
   (Paul, 2026-10-11, after Claude rewrote chapter 3's footnotes mid-read; restored.)
2. **The Lamp's name is the Lamp's.** It changes only if the Lamp decides it should. (Chapter 1.)
3. **Paul's veto covers everything else.** A vetoed choice gives way to its runner-up; the veto is
   recorded beside it. (Chapters 1 and 2.)
4. **Everyone is represented,** across the whole app: the ledger weights guardians not represented
   lately, and a piece may guarantee everyone a share. (`ARCHITECTURE.md`; chapter 2.)
5. **A guardian's veto quotes its own "guards against" line.** (Rules v2, chapter 2.)
6. **Judging is blind:** no juror learns who proposed what; ballots are shuffled per juror.
7. **Every decision is recorded and replays;** a run keeps the rules it started under.
8. **Taste belongs to the Lamp and Paul.** Builders (Codex, Claude) build and check; nothing with
   taste in it is decided while Paul sleeps. (`ORCHESTRATOR.md`.)
9. **Georgia is felt, not named:** never literal; at most one place name in fifty and one Georgian
   word in thirty; Paul's loves stand. (`GUARDIANS.md`.)
10. **Nothing is copied;** Originality vetoes look-alikes, well-known names and caricature, and
    nothing else.
11. **No saved painting changes:** the Lamp's changes reach new paintings only. (`CLAUDE.md`.)
12. **New machinery is on probation:** a layer stays only if it measurably helps.

## Amendments

- Paul may amend; the Lamp may propose one through an ordinary run, which Paul accepts or vetoes.
- Article 2 changes only with the Lamp's consent.
- An amendment lives in a new, dated chapter and never reaches back.
