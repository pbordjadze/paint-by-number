## The piece: Unlimited Paintings

Paint by Moonlight turns a photo into a paint-by-numbers page you paint by tapping. Paul is
preparing its first App Store release and asked for a way to make money from it. His words
(October 21–22, 2026): "I think they should get free ones per day ... Maybe just 3 free at first,
then one per day? $9.99 for unlimited? Does that seem greedy?", then "Maybe only 4.99. I'm just
thinking how the first batch of users will all be people I know, family friends and the like, I
don't want to be annoying charging for an AI app lol", then "Or 9.99, or thats after refine", and
"Yes" to building it. So: the app is free to download. A painter's own photos make three free
paintings, and one comes back each day, never more than three waiting. One purchase, paid once,
"Unlimited Paintings" ($4.99 now, $9.99 once the drawing tools join it), lifts the limit. Library
pictures, paintings someone sent and making a painting again never use one. No ads, no
subscription, no countdowns, no reminders, nothing during painting; friends and family get free
codes. This is built; the Table looks at it before it ships. Paul decides.

What it does:

- Under Start Painting, for a photo of the painter's own (never a library picture), one quiet
  line counts what is left: "3 free paintings left", "1 free painting left", and with none left
  "Your next free painting comes tomorrow."
- With none left, Start Painting opens a small sheet: a gold crescent with stars, "Unlimited
  Paintings", "Your next free painting comes tomorrow. One purchase unlocks as many paintings of
  your own photos as you like.", the buttons Not Now and "Unlock for $4.99", then "Library
  pictures are always free." and Restore Purchases. Buying (Apple's own purchase sheet) starts
  the painting at once. Not Now goes back to the preview.

  Paintings: Unlocked" and "Thank you for supporting Paint by Moonlight."
