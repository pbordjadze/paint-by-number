Moon, which you looked at as built this morning, went to Paul's own test build. Paul, trying it:
"Looks and sounds beautiful, but could be confusing—maybe this should just be a list of two music
options, and those can be “Sun” and “Moon” or “Auto”—and then auto can be default maybe? With Dawn
and Dusk to follow one day. If we can make that less confusing we can ship it."

So Settings' Sky menu (Moon, Sun or Follow the Sky, under it two sentences about music, stars and
hours) is now a plain list under the heading Music: Sun, Moon, Auto, a checkmark on the chosen
one, Auto by default, and one line under it: "Auto plays Sun from 6 AM and Moon from 7 PM, your
time." What each choice does is unchanged: by Sun each fill plays a bright kalimba note; by Moon a
lower bell with a quiet twinkle, and the two gold sparkles at a fill become stars. This is your
look before it ships to everyone: is it less confusing, and is it ready?
