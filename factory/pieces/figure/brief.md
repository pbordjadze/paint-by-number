Tune the little worker in the hero scene: its proportions, ink, gait, spell gesture,
particles and citation card. Judge the figure in motion and at its actual display size,
including light paper, dark paper and silhouette. Let your charter guide what you notice.

Proposals are nested JSON patches of FigureSpec fields, applied to the frozen
factory/specs/baseline.json. Omitted fields retain the baseline; arrays replace whole
arrays. Each patch has one line explaining the change. Fields and ranges come from
factory/SPEC.md; unknown fields, wrong types and values outside the ranges are rejected.
The engine's geometry and pixel checks must all pass before a candidate reaches the jury.

Jurors receive only shuffled letters and review sheets. Rank every letter once, give
reasons, and veto only by quoting your charter's guards-against line (rules v2).
Originality only vetoes. This piece supplies tools for a later Lamp run; canned dry-run
rankings demonstrate the plumbing and make no taste decision.
