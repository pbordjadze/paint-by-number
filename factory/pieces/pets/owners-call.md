# The owner's call: Tarragon

On October 12, after the maker jury rejected the Hedge Witch's pet, Paul asked for it to be
included anyway: "getting all of it out there, including that last pet." People decide the big
creative choices (`CONSTITUTION.md`, Who really decides), so his call stands over the jury's. The
jury's verdict stays in the record as it was.

- **What passed.** Codex's maker attempt (`hatches/maker-hedge-witch/attempt-3dn0pdku` in the
  F36 run), repackaged under the 2,000,000-byte limit (1,992,914 bytes), passed the atlas and
  quality validators and three blind direction reviews. Its leftward looks, which failed twice
  before, were redrawn and read correctly to every juror.
- **What the jury said.** Four of the six maker jurors (the Painter, Emily, the Nutcracker,
  George) asked for a redo on one ground: in the jumping row (row 4), the cap, body and basket are
  smaller than at idle, so Tarragon would change size between the two. Originality and the Open
  Source Enthusiast kept it. Under the commission's rules a redo meant withholding the pet.
- **In the app.** The Marginarium's player shows only idle (row 0) and waving (row 3), so the
  jumping row is not seen today. Before anything plays the jump (the walks in Paul's point 13),
  that row needs redrawing at idle's scale; that is Codex's to do.

Moon stays withheld, as Codex decided (`codex-lamp/makers-call.md`).
