Step 1, after Soyuzmultfilm's The Snow Queen (Moscow, 1955-1957): Hand-painted animation cel: ink and paint on celluloid over a painted background. A Soyuzmultfilm cel of 1957: flat, hand-inked and gouache-painted figures against a softly painted backdrop; the Queen as a tall, still, stone-pale figure in a long pale-blue and white gown and a high crown, face composed and cold, in a palace of glassy ice with a few hard geometric crystals.
