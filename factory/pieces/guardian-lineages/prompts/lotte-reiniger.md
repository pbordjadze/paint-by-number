Guardian: Scherenschnitt. Emblem to keep at every step: the same small plump bird in silhouette, standing upright on two thin legs, facing right, a crest of three feathers, a round eye hole, a short beak, wings attached at the shoulder by a hinge, lace-like cut-outs in the wing feathers. The outline must be the same at every step.

Step 0 (today, faithful to the approved drawing): the bird cut from matt black paper by hand with scissors, lying flat, its wings separate pieces fixed at the shoulder with small round brass pin hinges, the lace cuts in the feathers, the eye a clean round hole. Real paper fibre at the cut edges.
Step 1, after the Chat Noir shadow theatre, Paris 1886-1897: the same bird outline cut from thin zinc sheet with a fretsaw, lying flat: grey dull metal, slightly burred cut edges, the openwork cuts showing the backdrop through, the wing joined at the shoulder with a small rivet, a thin iron wire rod soldered on to hold it.
Step 2, after Séraphin's ombres chinoises, 1770s-1800: the same bird cut from solid thick pasteboard (layered card), blackened, its edges showing the grey card layers, a few small perforations, the wing a separate piece jointed with a twist of wire so it can move, mounted on a slender wooden stick. 18th-century handwork.
Step 3, Chinese shadow figure (piying): the same bird made as a traditional Chinese shadow figure: scraped translucent hide, intricate carved openwork in the feathers, dyed with transparent red, green and amber colours so light would show through, jointed at the shoulder with a knotted thread, a thin bamboo rod fixed at the neck. Real hide texture, slightly translucent on the grey backdrop.
