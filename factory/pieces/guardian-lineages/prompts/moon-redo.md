Guardian: the Moon. Emblem to keep: a crescent moon with its horns pointing left, a calm profile face looking right on its inner curve, and a small lamp hanging from the lower horn.

Step 1, the computus, England, late 11th to early 12th century: a vellum leaf from a reckoning book lying flat, with a ruled wheel of the moon's ages. Romanesque book-making, accurate to the period: all numbers in Roman numerals (i, ii, iii ... xxx, written in small minuscule with dots, as in medieval manuscripts), NO Arabic numerals anywhere; concentric rings ruled in red and black ink; small Latin words in a Caroline or early Gothic minuscule; light colour washes in green, red and ochre. At the hub, drawn in Romanesque pen outline with light wash, the crescent with its calm profile face and a small hanging lamp from its lower horn. Vellum with hair follicles, prick marks and slight cockling. No gold.
