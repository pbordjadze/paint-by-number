Every step is one photograph for the same museum catalogue, and all of them must match in presentation:
- Square canvas. The object alone, centred, filling about two thirds of the frame.
- The same seamless warm mid-grey paper backdrop (about #8a847c) in every image.
- The same light: soft and diffuse from the upper left, one gentle shadow falling to the lower right.
- Three-dimensional objects are shot straight on at object height; flat works (a page, a print, a cut-out) lie flat on the backdrop, shot from directly above, with a thin shadow at their edge.
- No text, captions, labels, signatures, frames, rulers, hands or people in the picture. (Any writing that is part of the object itself, like notation on a page, is fine.)
- Craft, not filter: it must look like a real photograph of the real physical thing, made in its own medium with that medium's actual tools, at true texture. Avoid the generic "ancient" look: no sepia wash, no added cracks, no gold leaf or glitter unless the medium really uses it.
- The guardian is an emblem, never a portrait: no likeness of any real person.
