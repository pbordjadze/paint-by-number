Guardian: Scherenschnitt. The shape: the bird's outline exactly (upright rounded body, crest at top, short pointed beak to the right, wing at the side, tail at lower left, two thin legs). Keep the OUTLINE only; inside it, nothing of a bird: no eye, no feathers, no wing hinges, no feather lace.
Step 1, after the Chat Noir shadow theatre, Paris 1886-1897: the outline cut from thin zinc sheet with a fretsaw, lying flat. Its interior is cut through in the Chat Noir's own scenery vocabulary: a fretwork of Montmartre rooftops, chimneys and a scatter of star holes. Dull grey metal, burred edges, a wire rod.
Step 2, after Séraphin's ombres chinoises, 1770s-1800: the outline cut from solid blackened pasteboard, with layered card showing at the edges, mounted on a slender wooden stick. Its interior is perforated with 18th-century French ornament: a rocaille scroll and pierced dots in neat rows. No feathers.
Step 3, Chinese shadow figure (piying): the outline made of scraped translucent hide, dyed in transparent red, green and amber, on a bamboo rod. Its interior is carved in traditional piying openwork: cloud scrolls, lattice and coin patterns, so light would pass through. No feathers, no eye.
