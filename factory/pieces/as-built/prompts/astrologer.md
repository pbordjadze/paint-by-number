## The piece: Sun and Moon, as built

Paint by Moonlight turns a photo into a paint-by-numbers page you paint by tapping. Sun and Moon is
a theme of its own, apart from dark mode (an accessibility setting) and the paper: Settings › Sky
offers Moon (the default, Paul's call), Sun, or Follow the Sky (Sun from 6 a.m. to 7 p.m. by the
phone's clock, Moon the rest). By Sun each fill plays a bright kalimba note; by Moon a celesta-like
bell on the same five notes, lower and minor, with a quiet twinkle where a phrase rests, and the two
gold sparkles that pop at a fill become eight-point stars with rounded points. Music and stars turn
only where a phrase begins. You decided the names, the hours, the sounds and the star in two rounds,
and asked at the first look for the stars to wait for the phrase and for rounder points: both done.
