# Chapter 6: the brief every guardian reads

Medea (Paint by Moonlight today) turns a photo into a paint-by-numbers painting you color with a
finger or a pencil. Every guardian of the Lamp has a pet: a small animated character, drawn as a
sprite sheet, that stands for its idea. Tonight the Lamp talks about the pets.

## Where the research is

- **The test pet.** Codex hatched a throwaway teapot with OpenAI's pet skill, entirely on our own
  machine, nothing uploaded. It took 21 image generations and about 18 minutes, and it passed every
  check. A pet sheet holds nine animations (idle, running right, running left, waving, jumping,
  failed, waiting, running, review) and sixteen directions the pet can look.
- **How each pet would be made.** One Codex session per pet, at most three at once. The Lamp
  writes a shared style sheet and reviews every image; Codex draws; Paul can veto any pet.
- **Not proven yet:** that nineteen pets drawn separately will look like one family. That is what
  the shared style sheet is for.
- **Already built:** the Lamp's collection page shows a pet's idle animation once its sheet
  exists, and holds still for people who turn motion off.

## The places a pet could live in the app

- **A** The Marginarium, the app's storybook: each guardian's page, beside its chapters and notes.
- **B** A companion in a corner while you paint, reacting now and then: waiting when you pause,
  jumping when a painting is finished.
- **C** Only at the finish: a pet celebrates a completed painting, then leaves.
- **D** The picture library: a pet brings you a picture to paint.
- **E** The making-of show: pets watch and cheer the stick-figure workers.
- **F** While a template is being made: the pet waits with you.
- **G** Alternate app icons: choose a guardian's pet as the app's icon.
- **H** Nowhere near the canvas: pets stay out of the painting screen.
