# Where two kinds of tips hang

Paint by Moonlight turns a photo into a paint-by-numbers page you paint by tapping. Its Gentle tips are small cards that each appear once, beside the thing they explain, with an arrow pointing at it. Checking every tip on iPhone and iPad, Claude found two places where a tip points at the wrong thing or hides what it is about, and Paul's answer was: "Fix them".

A. Three tips about the page itself (Zoom In on Small Areas, Paint Several Areas, Paint with Apple Pencil) hang from the top middle of the page area. On iPhone that spot sits just under the Show Photo button, so the arrow seems to point at Show Photo, which a different tip is about.
- A1 on the page: the tip hangs lower, its arrow touching the top of the drawing itself.
- A2 no arrow: the tip stays where it is but has no arrow; it is about the page, not a button.

B. Three tips about a button in the top bar (Settings and Tips at the gear, Make Your Own Page at +, Shape the Lines Yourself at Refine). On iOS 26 the system grows them out of the button itself, so the card covers the very button it is about (and, on iPhone, the screen's title), with no arrow.
- B1 below the button: an ordinary card hanging just under the button with an arrow, the button left in view (the card still covers what lies under it, as every tip card does).
- B now: keep the system's way.

The pictures are mock-ups made from CI's iPhone screenshots: the cards and words are the app's own, only moved; the shadows are approximate.
