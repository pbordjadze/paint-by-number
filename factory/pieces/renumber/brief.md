# Renumbering the book: the brief every guardian reads

Paul proposes that **chapter 3, "The Marginarium"**, become **chapter 1**: it is the night the
book got its name and the clearest door into the whole story. The two chapters before it move to
the back as appendices:

- Appendix A: How the Panel Named Itself (formerly chapter 1)
- Appendix B: Thirty-Six Colors (formerly chapter 2)
- Chapter 1: The Marginarium (formerly 3), then The Lamp Picks Up a Brush (2), The Lamp's
  Constitution (3), A Pet for Every Guardian (4), Two More Pets (5).

No chapter's words change, apart from a short note in each renumbered chapter saying it was
renumbered, and why. Paul will do this unless the Lamp objects strongly and unanimously, so say
plainly whether you object, and why.
