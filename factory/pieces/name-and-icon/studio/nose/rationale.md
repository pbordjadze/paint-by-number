# Rationale

**Name: keep; Moonlight on the home screen.** One idea, the quiet hour you paint in, naming the product in the rhythm of "paint by numbers". "Painted by moonlight" reads like a gift tag.

**Icon: keep the redesign.** The brand book calls paper, ink outline and numeral its core identity; the old icon buries them in a fifteen-shape landscape. Now one nameable object: the moon as the one area not yet painted, numbered 3, which is gold in the brand's paint box. It reads at 40 px, tinted too.

**Dark: changed.** v1 painted the moon gold; on a dark screen that read as any sleep app's moon. Now the numbered moon stays, in a deeper sky: one idea everywhere, the way Ffern holds to its solstices.

**Family and pets go to the screens**: a restrained mark, while paintings of your own people do the emotional work.
