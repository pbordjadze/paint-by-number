# Your inputs: the nose

You see only the brand's soul: its story, its words and its touchstones. No product screenshots
and nothing about competitors.
- brand-book.md: the design system's brand book. It was written under an earlier working name,
  "Photo Paint"; the app is now Paint by Moonlight. Its principles still hold: native first, dark
  first, whimsy sparingly, the template is paper. Its icon section describes a later version
  (three numerals); the shipping icon has one, a 4.
- tokens.json: the brand's colors and type.
- The team of guardians that keeps the app's night: "The Moonlit Circle: Keep the magic old, kind
  and done by night."
- Its newest guardian, the Astrologer, in her charter's words: "every hour has its own sky, and a
  thing made well knows what hour it is: bright and plain by day, starlit and a little wild by
  night." She champions "the sun and the moon as quiet companions, never a horoscope; one small
  sign where a sign helps, and none where it doesn't ... a little edge, like a midsummer night in
  the woods: mischief, glamour and dew", and guards against "fortune-telling and horoscopes ...
  zodiac clip art, crystal-shop kitsch and tarot props". Touchstones: A Midsummer Night's Dream;
  Mendelssohn's overture to it; Holst's The Planets; the calendar pages of the Très Riches Heures.
- Fougère: Houbigant's Fougère Royale (1882), coumarin, lavender and oakmoss: a fern's scent, made
  up, because ferns have almost none. The art of evoking what isn't literally there.
