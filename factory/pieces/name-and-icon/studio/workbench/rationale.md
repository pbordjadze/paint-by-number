**Name: keep, with Moonlight on the home screen. Icon: change, again.**

The phone preview showed my first draft as a tiny landscape among single-object icons; at 60 px it was clutter. So the icon is now one object, the moon, made the way the canvas makes things: ink only on its edge, its seas flat paint with no outline (as the coloring book paints), its highlands still paper, waiting for their 4 (the current icon's number). The seas are mostly round basins, so a few circles read as the moon even at 40 px and as a one-color silhouette. Three layers, five shapes.

Ffern's lesson is one idea held everywhere and restraint in the mark, with the art doing the feeling. So the moon carries the name and nothing more. Family and pets I leave to the screens: your own people, painted by you, are that art.
