App Store name: **Paint by Moonlight** (18 characters). Home-screen name: **Moonlight** (9). iOS cuts anything over 15, so the full name would show as "Paint by Moo…".

Why keep: the name gives the genre in two words, then an hour and a mood. The app names its paints the same way (Croissant, Tidal Flat, Corn Husk). It is one idea that the name, the icon and the evening habit of painting your own people can all carry. It is easy to say, easy to spell after hearing it once, and borrows from no one. On the home screen, "Moonlight" under a moon with a number in it is enough, since the App Store name has already said "paint". A game-streaming app is also called Moonlight on the home screen; the icon tells them apart.
