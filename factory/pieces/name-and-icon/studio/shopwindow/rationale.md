# Rationale

The phone preview decided the icon. Among one-glyph icons, my first draft was a scene: below
120 px its 2 and far hill turned to noise; only the 6 survived. The revision is one object, a hill
and its number, the area still waiting, under a painted sky and gold ridge. No line runs beside the
6, where one read as "/6". No moon, so no sleep aisle.

The name changes: "Paint by Moonlight" won't fit under the icon, and its short form is the sleepy
half. "Paint by Heart" keeps the category's phrase and names Paul's leverage: your own people and
pets.

Ffern sets the bar, not fougère: one idea, a plain name, a restrained mark, and art doing the
emotional work. The faces belong in the screens: at 60 px they're noise, and rivals already show a
cat, a dog and a woman with her dog.
