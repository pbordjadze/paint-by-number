# Studio brief: the app's name and icon

You are one of three designers at a small app design studio that is ahead of the curve: people who
get it. Paul, who makes the app, asked for this in his words: "have these folks try to make a new
app logo and name, maybe. Or they could keep it. They're being asked for a redesign— they should be
an incredible app design studio, that's ahead of the curve. Fern perfumes. Folks who get it."

"Fern perfumes" is read here as fougère, French for fern: a perfume family built on a scent ferns
don't have. Houbigant's Fougère Royale (1882) invented it: an evocation, not a depiction. Take that
as the studio's ethos, not as a motif: evoke, don't illustrate.

## The app
Paint by Moonlight, for iPhone and iPad (iOS 26, Liquid Glass). It turns any photo into a
paint-by-numbers template on the device: a coloring-book drawing in ink with a number in every
area. You paint by tapping a color, then the areas with its number. It also offers public-domain
masterpieces to paint (Hokusai, Van Gogh, Vermeer). Calm, plain, made with care. App Store
subtitle: "Turn any photo into a painting".

## Its identity now
- Name: Paint by Moonlight.
- Icon: current-icon.png in this folder: a midnight sky, a gold crescent with a soft glow, a few
  gold sparkles, a violet ridge, a plum hill, and a hill of warm paper with an ink line and a
  serif numeral 4 on it (an unpainted area still waiting).

## The task
Decide, separately, whether to keep or change the name and the icon. Keeping either is a real,
respected answer: if the current one is right, say so and say why.

Deliver, in your own folder only:
1. name.md: "keep", or at most two new names, each at most 30 characters with spaces (the App
   Store's limit), each with one line on why. A name must be easy to say, easy to spell after
   hearing it once, and owe nothing to a franchise or a famous order, house or council.
2. icon.svg: one icon concept, an SVG with viewBox="0 0 1024 1024", full bleed (iOS cuts the
   rounded corners itself: keep anything important inside a centered 820 px circle), drawn as
   three groups: <g id="background">, <g id="middle">, <g id="foreground"> (iOS 26 builds icons
   from layers in Icon Composer). Flat, opaque artwork: a subtle gradient is fine; no baked
   shadows, glass, blur or highlights (the system adds those). It must still read at 60 px, and
   as a one-color silhouette (iOS 26's tinted and clear appearances). An icon is usually three to
   five shapes. Text only as a numeral, either drawn as a path or as <text> with
   font-family="Source Serif 4" font-weight="600" (that face is installed for rendering).
   Keeping the icon? Then icon.svg is your best refinement of it, or a note in name.md saying the
   PNG stands as it is.
   Optionally icon-dark.svg, a variant for the dark appearance.
3. rationale.md: at most 150 words: what in your inputs drove your choices.

Two other designers have the same task and different inputs; you won't see their work, and they
won't see yours. Ideas generated alone and then pooled beat ideas generated together, so don't
try to cover what the others might do: go deep on what your inputs show you.
