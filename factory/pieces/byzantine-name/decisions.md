# A name for the Byzantine workshop: decisions

October 23, 2026. Paul: "pick the ones who already are ancient and have them name the new
branch, please. But like, don't overdo it." One round, three seats from the Moonlit Circle's
oldest (Medea, the Moon, the Astrologer) and Originality's veto; blind ballots, charters word for
word, each seat a Claude subagent (opus, low effort) that read only its prompt and the brief.

Ravenna is on every ballot; Andamento is Medea's first. Originality vetoed nothing.

## Why, in their words

- Medea (Andamento): "names the craft itself, the deliberate flow of rows a skilled hand lays,
  so the photo turns into a mosaic you can watch being set row by row."
- The Moon (Ravenna): "the place where gold glows out of the dark inside San Vitale, one warm
  light in a stone nave, a quiet night chapel to work in while the painter waits."
- The Astrologer (Gold Ground): "the daylight sister to Moonlight: you can tell the hour from it,
  gold that glows by candle and sun, with the sky only hinted at."

## Cautions

- Originality: avoid leaning on the bare word "Mosaic", a famous early web browser and a crowded
  product name.
- The Moon: don't let the gold ground spread over every screen; keep the dark nave, and let the
  gold catch the light only where a tile is being set.
- Medea: no name that is only glitter, gold or sparkle without the craft behind it.
- The Astrologer: not a name that reads as a date the clock is fated to stop at, like 547.

## Decision

The Table's proposal is Ravenna (slug `ravenna`), Andamento close behind. Paul decides.
