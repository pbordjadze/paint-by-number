# Day and Night: what was decided

The Lamplit Table's first piece (October 17, 2026). Six seats, each a low-effort Opus subagent
given its charter word for word, the brief and nothing else; every prompt and reply is beside
this file. Claude drafted the Astrologer's and Madeleine's charters; both kept them as drafted.

**Round 1 (`brief.md`, all six seats).** No vetoes.

| Question | Decided | The count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Names | Sun, Moon; automatic: Follow the Sky | Sun and Moon 5 of 5; Follow the Sky 3, By the Sky 1 (the Hedge Witch), By the Hour 1 (Rikyū) |
| Hours | Sun from 6 a.m., Moon from 7 p.m., local time, all year | 6 a.m. 3 to 2 (the Child at Bedtime and Madeleine said 7); 7 p.m. 4 to 1 (Rikyū said 6) |
| Sun's sound | today's kalimba and tune, a little brighter and lighter | 5 of 5 |
| Moon's voice | a celesta-like bell | 3; a music box 2 (the Child at Bedtime, the Hedge Witch) |
| Moon's scale | the day's five notes, lower, as their minor | 5 of 5 |
| Twinkle | yes, a quiet echo above some notes | 4; Rikyū no ("one voice per tap is enough") |
| Visual nod | by Moon the fill's sparkles become stars | 4; Rikyū: nothing beyond Settings |
| Finale | differs by sky | 5 of 5 |

Cautions taken: no glass harmonica and no half-step scale at night (the Child at Bedtime); never
spooky (Madeleine); not glassy or perfectly clean (the Hedge Witch: the bell is a little uneven);
nothing that tells the painter about themselves (the Astrologer); no name after a famous order,
house or council, and no well-known music-box or film melody (Originality). The Astrologer sits
with the Moonlit Circle, her choice.

**Round 2 (`round-2.md`, all six seats).** Claude's reading of round 1, approved by all six, no
vetoes. The twinkle sounds only where a phrase comes to rest (Rikyū's caution). The night star is
an eight-point star, like one on an old sky chart (the Astrologer, the Hedge Witch, Madeleine;
the Child at Bedtime and Rikyū preferred the sparkle with a dot beside it), drawn a touch uneven
(the Hedge Witch) and close to the sparkle (Rikyū). The jury's name, by ranking (6 points for a
first place down to 1): The Lamplit Table 24 (Originality's proposal; first for the Hedge Witch
and Madeleine), The Lantern Circle 20, The Night-Light Circle 20, The Turning Sky 16, The Circle
of Quiet 14 (Madeleine ranked her own book title third), The Quiet Room 11.

**Paul's call (October 17, after both rounds).** "Honestly — moon vs sun needs to be a little
separate from dark or light mode — one is accessibility, one is a theme, and it should also be
allowed to just stay in moon or sun — moon should be default, with sun or “auto” as options".
Moon is the default and comes first (Moon, Sun, Follow the Sky); the Table had only named the
choices, and Claude's brief had proposed the automatic one as the default. Sky has a Settings
section of its own, a theme apart from the system's appearance and from the paper. The automatic
choice keeps the Table's name, Follow the Sky: Settings › Paper already has an Automatic, which
follows dark mode.

**Built** on `claude/day-night-music-astrological-theme-ycqyrm` (the app's `Sky`, Settings › Sky,
`SoundPlayer`, `ToneSynth`, `PaintingMelody`, `Sparkle.starPath`). Claude's own calls, for the
Table to look at: Sun a tone above the old C (D major pentatonic, "a touch higher"), Moon from the
B below it (the same notes); the Moon's flourish slower, its finale a run down to B, D, F♯ and A
with B an octave up left ringing; the Settings row's icon shows the sky that holds now.
