# Day and Night, round 2

Round 1 heard all six seats (the brief: `brief.md` beside this file). No vetoes. This is what
Claude made of it; say if it betrays your charter, then rank the names.

## What round 1 decided

- **Names** (all five who vote): **Sun** and **Moon**. The automatic choice: **Follow the Sky**
  (three of five; "By the Sky" and "By the Hour" one each). Automatic is the default.
- **The hours**: Sun from **6 a.m.** (three of five; two said 7), Moon from **7 p.m.** (four of
  five; one said 6), the painter's own local time, all year. A change waits for the tune's next
  phrase.
- **Sun's sound** (all five): today's kalimba and today's tune, a little brighter and lighter:
  a touch higher, shorter notes, more silence between them.
- **Moon's sound**: a celesta-like bell (three of five; two said a music box), on the same five
  notes as the day, set lower as their minor (all five), a little uneven, as if made by hand (the
  Hedge Witch), never glassy or eerie (the Child at Bedtime, Madeleine). Some notes twinkle, a
  quiet echo above them, like a star (four of five), but not every note: one voice per tap,
  the twinkle only where a phrase comes to rest (Rikyū's caution).
- **The visual nod** (four of five; Rikyū said nothing beyond Settings): by Moon, the two gold
  sparkles that pop when an area fills become two small gold stars. By Sun they stay as they are.
  In Settings each choice has its symbol, and the row shows the sky that holds now, a sun or a
  crescent with stars.
- **The finale** differs (all five). Sun: a bright run up to an open, sunlit chord, then quiet.
  Moon: a slower, softer run that settles on a warm low chord, with one high star-note left
  ringing above it.
- **Originality's cautions**: no name after a famous order, house or council, and no
  well-known music-box or film melody. The tunes are our own.
